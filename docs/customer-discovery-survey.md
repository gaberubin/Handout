# Handout — Customer Discovery Survey & Interview Guide

> Method note (per Dr. Mike / NSF I-Corps): this is about **learning the problem**, not
> selling Handout. Questions ask about real *past behavior*, never "would you use my app."
> Do NOT substitute AI-generated answers for real people — collect real responses only.

**Survey intro (Google Forms):**
> "I'm a Rowan student doing research on student life — takes 3 min, not selling anything. Thanks!"

## A. About you (screening)
1. Are you a current or incoming college student? (Yes, current / Incoming / No)
2. Which school? ____
3. Year (Freshman / Sophomore / Junior / Senior / Grad / Transfer / Incoming)
4. Domestic or international student?
5. This coming semester you'll live: On-campus / Off-campus / Still figuring it out

## B. Roommates & housing
6. In the last 12 months, did you look for a roommate or a place to live near campus? (Yes/No)
7. If yes — how did you look? (Facebook groups / GroupMe / friends / Zillow / school housing / other)
8. How frustrating was that, 1 (easy) – 5 (nightmare)?
9. Describe the last time you looked for housing or a roommate — the most annoying part? (open text)

## C. Buying & selling
10. In the last year, did you buy or sell textbooks / dorm stuff? Where?
11. What went wrong, if anything? (open text)

## D. Meeting people
12. Early in a semester, how do you actually meet people / find study or gym buddies?
13. How hard is that, 1–5?

## E. Priority + follow-up
14. Of everything above, what's the single biggest hassle of student life for you? (open text)
15. Open to a quick 10-min chat about this? Drop your email (optional): ____

## Interview questions (richer than surveys — expect ~5% response)
1. Walk me through the last time you looked for a roommate/sublet. Start to finish.
2. What did you try? What did you use before that?
3. What was the most frustrating moment?
4. How did you eventually solve it (or didn't you)?
5. If you could wave a wand, what would've made it easy?
6. Who else deals with this worse than you?

*(None mention Handout — you're learning about the problem, not pitching.)*

## Where to find real respondents (5% response rate is normal)
- r/Rowan, Rowan Class-of GroupMes / Facebook groups
- Dorm / class group chats (ask friends to drop the link)
- Tabling in the student center / library with a QR to the form
- The "10-min chat?" emails from survey Q15 → your interviews
